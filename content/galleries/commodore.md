Before I forget, here are the different issues with the monitor. After the recap, I reflowed the board, but that didn’t solve the problem. The real problem was the transistor on the green channel, which was completely non‑functional. It caused non‑diode‑like effects with the variable resistor for the green channel. Additionally, the spark gap for the green channel was letting some voltage through, which probably caused the transistor to overheat and the green channel to drop out.

All the transistors were replaced, and the spark gap for the green channel was removed. I removed the spark gaps for all the channels, but I’ll put them back in since they didn’t seem to cause any adverse effects.

The next problem was a missing resistor on the brightness circuit. I reinstated it, and now I can adjust the brightness properly.

Finally, the weird brightness issue was due to my dumb ass wiring the G2 voltage to the G1 voltage. The G2 was only changing the focus, not the screen brightness, which caused it to be over‑flooded.

These were all the problems I solved to fix the monitor.
